Glass recipe: card is blur(26px) saturate(150%) over a 155° translucent gradient (11% → 3.5%), border brighter on top than bottom to imply a light source, plus a diagonal specular sweep and an inset top highlight. Inputs go the other way — recessed wells with an inner shadow — so the eye reads card above, input below. Grain overlay stops the big blurred gradients banding on dark screens.

Deliberately only two blur layers. Nesting backdrop-filter on every field would cost real frames on mobile and mud the colour; the field cards are plain translucency sitting on already-blurred glass, which is visually identical here.
